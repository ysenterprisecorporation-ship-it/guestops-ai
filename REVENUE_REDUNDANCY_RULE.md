# Revenue Redundancy Rule — 3+ Parallel Lanes

## Core rule
For every revenue goal, never rely on a single acquisition or conversion path.
Maintain at least 3 independent revenue lanes in parallel and aim to make more than one lane succeed before the deadline.

## Priority 1: Projects ①②③

### Lane A — Direct outbound sales
- expand qualified lead inventory continuously
- personalize outreach
- track delivered -> reply -> meeting -> order -> profit
- replace failed/bounced contacts with alternate official channels
- never stop at the current lead count

### Lane B — SNS inbound sales
- publish problem-recognition, self-diagnosis, before/after, mini-template, and proof-oriented posts
- move users to pinned diagnostic / profile action / existing Square offer
- classify comments/inquiries and generate reply drafts
- track reach -> profile/action -> inquiry -> Square -> order -> profit

### Lane C — Existing-buyer expansion / upsell
- A/P3/P10/P30/monthly ladder
- identify the next-best offer for each buyer
- trigger upgrade guidance based on purchased product and operational need
- measure upgrade revenue and recurring revenue separately

### Lane D — Partner / referral channel
- target operators, consultants, property managers, DX firms, hotel-management firms, and related partners
- offer a collaboration or packaged implementation where fit is verified
- track partner leads separately from direct buyers

## Priority 2: Project ④

Maintain at least 3 revenue/occupancy defenses:
1. OTA optimization / group-stay positioning
2. direct booking / repeat / referral route
3. monthly or other legally valid non-minpaku occupancy route for restricted periods

## Operating rules
- A lane is not "alive" unless it has current actions, measurable funnel stages, and a next execution step.
- Do not wait for replies from one lane before advancing the others.
- If one lane underperforms, increase volume or improve conversion on the remaining lanes immediately.
- Aim to achieve the target before the formal deadline; use remaining time as upside, not as required runway.
- Track revenue and profit by lane so weak channels can be cut and strong channels scaled.
