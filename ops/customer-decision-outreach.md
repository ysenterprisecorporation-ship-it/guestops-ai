# Customer Decision Outreach Guide

Use only verified company facts supplied as input.

## Decision sequence
1. Show why the message is relevant using one verified observation.
2. Ask one operational question that helps the recipient identify a real problem.
3. Explain the operational cost or risk without unsupported numbers.
4. Describe the concrete deliverable or mechanism.
5. Ask for one small next action.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent scarcity, client results, logos, awards, or numerical proof.
- Use documented evidence only.
- Keep the first requested action small and easy to answer.
- Offer a useful sample or diagnostic before asking for a larger commitment.

## Output fields
company_name
verified_observation
problem_question
risk_statement
mechanism
next_action
subject
body
