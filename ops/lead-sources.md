# Authoritative Lead Sources for ①②

## Primary source
MLIT Residential Accommodation Management Business registry.
Use as the first-party source of registered operators:
- National search: https://etsuran2.mlit.go.jp/TAKKEN/jutakuKensaku.do
- Registry portal: https://www.mlit.go.jp/totikensangyo/const/sosei_const_tk3_000140.html

## Use
For each operator:
1. capture legal company name and registry evidence
2. resolve official corporate website
3. confirm accommodation/minpaku management activity
4. locate official business-contact channel
5. score fit
6. deduplicate against prior outreach
7. create a personalized draft
8. leave outbound status as draft until approved

## Priority scoring
+30 multiple facilities / operation outsourcing
+20 24h guest support / remote operation
+20 cleaning / maintenance / emergency response
+15 owner reporting / property management
+15 staff training / standardization need

Subtract:
-25 no official business contact
-20 contact is explicitly guest-reservation-only
-15 unclear current operation evidence

## Expansion sources
After registry-qualified operators, expand to:
- hotel management contractors
- vacation rental operators
- unmanned / labor-saving accommodation operators
- property managers that also run accommodation
- cleaning and facility-management firms with accommodation operations

Do not treat a registry listing alone as permission to send. Verify a suitable official business contact channel.
