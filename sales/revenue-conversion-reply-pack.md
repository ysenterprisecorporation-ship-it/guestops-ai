## Follow-up rule

- A real reply has priority over new cold outreach.
- Keep the next action small.
- Do not push a meeting unless the prospect asks or the deal requires synchronous discussion.
- Do not claim ROI, savings, or client results without evidence.
- For ①②, preserve the approved price ladder.
- For ③, preserve direct-sale / partner strategy and do not route SNS traffic to Coconala.
