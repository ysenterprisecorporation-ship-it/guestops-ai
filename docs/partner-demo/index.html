The problem we solve

1. Missing PMS booking

An OTA booking exists, but a matching PMS record is absent. We flag the exception instead of calling the night available.

2. Changed or cancelled booking

Different sources disagree about dates or status. We expose the conflicting evidence and identify manual verification steps.

3. Same-day turnover

Departure and next arrival fall on the same day. We identify the cleaning handoff and avoid treating checkout as an occupied night.
